A Correspondence · 8-minute read by candlelight

Letters from the Moon

A curious correspondence from beyond our skies.

A series of enigmatic letters, said to have come from the Moon itself, has arrived at the Night Post House on Wick Street, written on paper unlike any found in the mortal world. Their messages are brief but profound, and every one is signed with a single silver star.

By Orion Belle · Keeper of Undeliverable Letters, the Night Post House

Inside the Brushmaker’s Workshop

Where wood remembers and brushes choose.

Behind a nondescript door at the end of the lane lies a workshop where patience and intuition are carved into every handle. One frame-mender found the door that no map shows, and came home with a brush that remembers a name.

By Mara Kingsley · Frame-mender of Lantern Row

A Night Report · 10-minute read by candlelight

The Night the Portraits Stepped Out

What the night reveals when the crowds are gone.

When the last shutters close and the last visitor departs, another side of the city emerges. A young night-watchman spends his first night on watch, the longest of the year, among the portraits, and learns the stories they only tell after midnight.

By Jasper Cale · Apprentice Night-Watchman, the Galerie des Vernis
