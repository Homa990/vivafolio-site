Overheard This Week

Sir Viridian underlined a rival’s review. Twice.

Dame Lazuli swears the moon portrait winked.

Maestro Carmine has filed a complaint about dust.

Visitors to the east gallery swear Dame Lazuli has changed her pose three times since Tuesday. First she looked out of the window. Then she looked straight at us.

This morning, according to the night guard, she was simply gone — leaving behind an empty velvet chair and a faint smell of linseed oil.

Our critics are divided. Is this a masterpiece of restless brushwork, or did she simply need a stretch? Turn to Chuchoté for the frames’ own version of events.
